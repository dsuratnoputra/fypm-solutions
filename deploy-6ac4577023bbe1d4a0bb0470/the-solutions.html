Eight deal dimensions

Every verdict covers the same eight questions, so deals can be compared side by side.

  • 01

    Ownership & structure

    Foreign-ownership limits, nominee risk and the vehicle that actually works.

  • 02

    Permits & approvals

    Which licences, from whom, in what order, and how long they really take.

  • 03

    Power & infrastructure

    Grid capacity, connection timelines, logistics and site readiness.

  • 04

    Data & sector rules

    Data residency, sector-specific regulation and local-content requirements.

  • 05

    Tax & incentives

    Effective tax position, holidays and allowances, and treaty routes.

  • 06

    Forex & repatriation

    Getting capital and returns in and out, and what it costs to do so.

  • 07

    Labour & hiring

    Expatriate permits, local hiring rules and the depth of the talent market.

  • 08

    Geopolitical climate

    Policy direction, election cycles and exposure to external shocks.

Every claim, sourced.

Each finding in a report carries a source tag and a “last checked” date, so you always know what is official, what is reported, what is the engine's estimate — and what still needs someone on the ground.

  • Official

    Government regulation, gazette, ministry or regulator publication.

  • Reported

    Credible press, industry or analyst reporting — not yet confirmed officially.

  • AI estimate

    The engine's inference where no direct source exists. Always marked, never presented as fact.

  • FYPM verified

    Confirmed in-country by an FYPM team member, with the date it was checked.

  • Needs field check

    Material to the verdict and unconfirmed. Eligible for escalation to fieldwork.
