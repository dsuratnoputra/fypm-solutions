The Firm

Independent. Senior. In‑country.

FYPM is an independent advisory firm for investors in Indonesia. Our senior executives have spent decades running businesses here — and they are hands-on in every engagement we take.

Background

We help investors solve the business problems Indonesia throws at them.

Incorporated in Singapore and run from Jakarta, FYPM provides comprehensive advisory services to local and international investors on their most complex issues — business strategy, commercial and operational performance, Project Management Office (PMO) discipline, and exit execution.

We are client-centric, with a single-minded focus on the Indonesian market. In-depth knowledge of technology, media and telecommunications (TMT), and broad execution capability across a wide range of transactions, set us apart. Strong relationships in adjacent industries let our clients explore cross-industry opportunities.

Our team blends specialisations from different industries and functional backgrounds. We use that range to guide management teams towards higher levels of performance.

Public-sector mandateAppointed by Indonesia's Ministry of Tourism and Creative Economy through 2017 and 2018 to bring private investment into the country's “Top 10” priority tourism destinations.
